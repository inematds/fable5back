What this course is

Claude Fable 5 is the strongest—and most expensive—model in Anthropic’s current lineup. It became available globally again on July 1, 2026, after a temporary suspension related to U.S. government export controls. This course teaches you to prompt this specific model well, not using Claude in general.

The content cross-checks a video lesson with 6 practical techniques against Anthropic’s official documentation, citing the official text whenever there’s a verifiable citation — and clearly marking when something is general prompt engineering practice, not a literal quote from the Fable 5 docs.

🗓️ Quick context: why this course now

Fable 5 and Mythos 5 launched on June 9, 2026. On June 12, the U.S. government imposed export controls on both models, and because nationality couldn’t be verified in real time, Anthropic suspended access for ALL users. The real reason: Amazon researchers reported a way to bypass Fable 5’s safeguards—but Anthropic’s own test confirmed that much less capable models (Opus 4.8, GPT-5.5, Kimi K2.7, and even Haiku 4.5, Sonnet 4.6, and Opus 4.6/4.7) could produce the same exploit demonstration. This wasn’t a capability unique to Fable 5—the suspension was a regulatory precaution. On July 1, 2026 (today), both models are returning globally.

💡 How I decide which model to use

This corresponds to how I manage the process day to day. I use the Opus for the architecture stage, the Sonnet when the task is well defined, and Fable when the task is complex and needs its own investigation cycle.
